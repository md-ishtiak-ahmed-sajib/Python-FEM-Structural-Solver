# Explain authorship and AI assistance

[Project home](../../README.md) · [Documentation map](../README.md) · [Section guide](README.md)


Good engineering work includes an honest record of who did what.

## What is recorded now

The owner chose the project purpose, scope, research direction, local-computer approach and evidence requirements.

The initial code and documents were produced with substantial AI assistance through Codex. That assistance included implementation, tests, explanations and debugging. The documentation organization and plain-English rewrite also used AI assistance.

The guided learning stages, terminology source, contextual explanations, comparison tools, README equations and original diagrams also used AI assistance. The owner selected the learning goals and reviewed plan; no independent owner implementation or physical validation is implied.

Do not describe this starting implementation as independently written by the owner without assistance.

## How the owner can build a clear personal contribution

Review the code and equations. Repeat results yourself. Record corrections, new derivations, new examples, design decisions, real experiments and your own interpretation.

Keep dated notes that distinguish existing generated work from later personal work. Do not claim that a review or experiment happened until it did.

## What to disclose when sharing

Explain which parts you designed, wrote, checked or changed. Name the numerical libraries and cite the theory. State the role of AI and the steps used to check its output.

Contributors should describe their own assistance and sources too. Do not invent faculty feedback, collaborators, publications or physical data.

## Names and citation

The [citation file](../../CITATION.cff) uses the public name connected to the owner's chosen GitHub account. The assistance record above remains part of the repository so that citation and implementation history are both clear.

## Academic presentation

Show what you understand, what you contributed, how you checked it and what remains uncertain. Follow the actual application's rules for outside assistance and supplementary material.
